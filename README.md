# CardBuddy 🔴⚪

> Your AI card-shop buddy — point your camera at a Pokémon card and it tells you what it is, and what it's worth.

## ElevenHacks

CardBuddy was built for the **ElevenHacks** hackathon, integrating **ElevenLabs** and **D-ID**.

## About

CardBuddy is a real-time AI agent that answers your questions about Pokémon cards through live, natural audio conversations. It can **see your cards through your webcam** — hold one up and it identifies the card, talks about the set and the Pokémon, and tells you the current market value, all out loud.

To make the experience feel genuinely alive, CardBuddy uses **D-ID live avatars** — a digital human that watches, listens, and responds in real time, turning a simple price lookup into an immersive, face-to-face conversation.
